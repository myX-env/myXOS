[Development Environment]
Windows 10
.NET Framework 4.8

[Overview]
This tool allows you to invert the colors of a selected area of your screen in real time.
The display box can be moved and resized, making it easier to focus on a specific part of your workspace.

[How to Use]
Launch myXXorBox.exe.
Drag the display box to move it.
Drag the edges or corners to resize the box.
The area inside the box will be inverted in real time.
Right-click to open the context menu.

[Right-Click Menu]
Back (Cancel)
Minimize
Drawing ON
Drawing OFF
25 fps (40 ms)
15 fps (67 ms)
2 fps (500 ms, default)
Custom Value (ms)
Exit

[Custom Value]
You can specify a custom update interval in milliseconds.
Minimum allowed value: 10 ms
Values 9 ms or lower cannot be used.

[Notes]
Shorter update intervals may increase CPU usage.
When Drawing OFF is selected, the inversion process stops.
